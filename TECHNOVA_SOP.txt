=> DIGITAL SIGNATURE

Flow :-
--------
----> JOB STATUS FOR DIGISIGNATURE

Night DIGISINATURE JOB time : 5.00 AM everyday (automatic)
Day DIGISINATURE JOB time : Every 2-3 mins as per requirement (automatic).

The DIGISINATURE JOB is run by JDE Team on JDE APP. Below are the stages of the job.
0. W (waiting)
1. S (In Queue)
2. P (pending)
3. G (picked for signature)
		|
		--------- (waiting between G & D)
		|
4. D (done)

The entire process between the job status "G" & "D" is explained below.

1. DAY & NIGHT DIGITAL SIGNATURE reports of both TISPL & TPPPL are getting generated on enterprise servers BP01 & BP02 by the DIGISINATURE JOB.
BP01 - TPPPL
BP02 - TISPL
	(Note:- When the reports are sent to destination server they are deleted from BP01/02).
2. For getting digitally verfied the reports are picked from BP01/02 and placed to DIGISIGNATURE servers in (Archieve-Original) folder with the help of windows TASK SCHEDULER JOB.
3. Once the reports are present in the DIGISIGNATURE servers they are DIGITALLY verified with the help of same windows TASK SCHEDULER JOB and copied in (Archieve-DigiSign) folder.
4. After getting verified the reports are mailed to the customers (only night reports are mailed not day).
	If night then cross verify the mails sent to vendors and the reports generated in (Archieve-DigiSign) folder.
	If day then check whether anything pending in BP01/02.
	If a particular report is not digitally verified then copy the report from the destination server and paste it again in the BP01/02 and tell darly mam to run the 
	windows TASK SCHEDULER JOB again.
5.	If the reports are generated properly and sent to DIGISIGNATURE servers and digitally signed but the mail not sent then tell JDE team to update the mail parameter of DIGISINATURE JOB 
	and rerun the job. Now check whether the reports are getting generated at BP01/02 and then run the windows TASK SCHEDULER JOB and check whether picked by the windows 
	TASK SCHEDULER JOB or not and check the destination, mail & logs simultaneously.
6. If jobs not running properly reports not getting generated then go to windows TASK SCHEDULER JOB on DIGISIGNATURE server => open Task scheduler => on left select digital signature 
	folder => right click run => check the logs for any error in logs folder(D:\TPPPL-DigitalSignature-9.2\DigitalSignature-Night\logs).

Once the entire process is completed the DIGISINATURE JOB status will be "D".
